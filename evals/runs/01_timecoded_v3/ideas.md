# Step 2 - raw hook ideas for B03 (hook only, must read into "Every morning I dig through a basket of strangers.")

1. ~~Where does the other sock go?~~ - obvious mystery opener, every batch has it.
2. ~~My dryer runs a sock smuggling ring.~~ - the stock humor line named in SKILL.md.
3. ~~How many single socks are in your basket?~~ - the stock question line.
4. ~~It's 7:45 and I'm missing a sock.~~ - the winner's own hook retold.
5. I own 40 socks and zero pairs. -> **B03-V01** (math)
6. ~~I counted 41 single socks this morning.~~ - too close to #5, keep one math hook.
7. Confession: I wore two different socks to work. -> **B03-V02** (confession)
8. ~~I've gone to a meeting in two different socks.~~ - same idea as #7.
9. I didn't think socks could fix my mornings. -> **B03-V04** (skeptic)
10. Three kids, six loads, zero matching socks. -> **B03-V05** (parent)
11. ~~Sunday laundry: 6 loads, 3 matching pairs.~~ - overlaps #10 and #5.
12. My sock basket needs a missing persons unit. -> **B03-V07** (humor)
13. Today I interviewed every sock in my basket. -> **B03-V06** (humor; "strangers" in scene_1 pays it off)
14. ~~Do you also own a basket of strangers?~~ - steals scene_1's own line, scene_1 would then repeat it.
15. When did you last wear a matching pair? -> **B03-V08** (question)
16. My husband thinks I lose socks on purpose. -> **B03-V09** (relationship)
17. ~~My partner blames me for every missing sock.~~ - same as #16, weaker.
18. ~~This saved me ten minutes every morning.~~ - contradicts present-tense "Every morning I dig..." (flow risk).
19. My worst morning habit had a silly fix. -> **B03-V10** (hack; scene_1 describes the habit, scene_2 the fix)
20. Socks as a gift? Hear me out. -> **B03-V11** (gift; pays off in "my sister sent me socks")
21. ~~I laughed when my sister sent socks.~~ - gift arrives before scene_1, then "Then my sister sent me socks" repeats it out of order.
22. ~~The bus is honking. I'm holding one sock.~~ - same moment as the winner's hook (one sock, late).
23. Late again, and it's the socks' fault. -> **B03-V12** (morning-rush, blame instead of a clock)
24. ~~Three minutes to leave. Zero matching socks.~~ - near the winner's hook and #10.
25. Breaking: another sock vanished from this house overnight. -> **B03-V14** (own: news-report)
26. Things I lose daily: keys, patience, socks. -> **B03-V15** (own: list)
27. I dare you to find one pair here. -> **B03-V16** (own: dare)
28. ~~My dryer and I are not speaking.~~ - dryer villain is the stock joke again.
29. Remember when socks came in pairs? -> **B03-V17** (own: nostalgia)
30. ~~My teenager says socks are a scam.~~ - different speaker from the body's narrator.
31. ~~Night shift over, now find a sock.~~ - clashes with "every morning", different speaker.
32. ~~My dog only steals the left one.~~ - broken promise: no dog in the body (flow.md example).
33. Packing for a trip. Found eleven single socks. -> **B03-V18** (own: trip, NEW shot)
34. ~~First date tonight. Socks don't match.~~ - promise (the date) the body never pays off.
35. ~~One sock always stays in the locker room.~~ - the gym-bag example from SKILL.md, also not paid off.
36. Moved apartments. Found 23 socks. Zero pairs. -> **B03-V19** (own: moving)
37. One star for my sock basket. Here's why. -> **B03-V20** (own: review)
38. ~~I haven't matched socks in a month.~~ - contradicts "Every morning I dig...".
39. ~~My sock drawer finally lost the war.~~ - implies it is solved, contradicts scene_1.
40. I'm a grown adult who can't find socks. -> **B03-V03** (confession)
41. ~~Spent $60 on socks this year. Still one.~~ - price outside the CTA.
42. Socks go in as pairs. Never come out. -> **B03-V13** (mystery; scene_3 "still a pair" answers it)
43. ~~Hopping down the hallway in one sock. Again.~~ - just describes the winner's hook shot.
44. ~~After a sleepover, nobody's socks match.~~ - sleepover never paid off, and other kids' socks muddle the story.
