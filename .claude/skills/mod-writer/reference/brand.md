# SockLock brand brief

## Product
Magnetic socks that never get lost in the laundry. A tiny magnet sewn into each cuff keeps the pair
together: you take them off, snap the cuffs together, toss them in the wash, and they come out as a pair.
Soft cotton blend, machine-washable and dryer-safe; the magnet is sealed inside the cuff.

## Offer (use exactly, never inflate)
- Subscription: 5 pairs for $29.
- A new delivery every quarter (every 3 months).
- Shipping: 2 days in the US.
- Cancel anytime.
- Entry point: a short quiz (size + style) that leads to a personal offer.

## Audience (US, comes from Meta ads)
- Busy parents doing 5+ loads of laundry a week; the "sock basket of shame" lives in their house.
- Young professionals in a morning rush who keep finding one sock.
- People who hate chores and love small life hacks.
- Gift buyers looking for a useful, slightly funny present.

## Pains in their own words
- "Where does the other sock go? Every. Single. Time."
- "I have a whole basket of single socks."
- "I'm late and I can't find a matching pair."
- "I keep buying new socks and they still disappear."
- "Folding and matching socks is the worst part of laundry."

## Tone of voice
- Conversational, first person, a bit of humor; sounds like a friend, not an ad.
- Short sentences. Concrete everyday moments (dryer, laundry basket, 7:45 a.m.).
- Confident, but no hype and no absolute promises.
- English (US). Contractions are fine. No emoji in VO.

## Hard rules (check.py enforces these)
- On-screen text: at most 5 words in any scene.
- Never use: "guaranteed", "forever", "100%".
- Price ($29, "29 dollars") only in the CTA.
- The hook must not reveal the product: no "magnet", "magnetic", "click" in the hook.
  The reveal happens in the body.
- Hook VO: at most 8 words (about 3 seconds).
- VO of other scenes: not longer than the original line.
- Shots only from the winner script itself. A shot that needs filming is written "NEW: <scene>" and marked
  "New footage needed".

## Soft rules
- Lead with the problem or a curiosity gap, not with the brand name.
- Do not mock the viewer; laugh with them.
- No medical, eco or durability claims we cannot prove ("lasts 10 years", "eco-friendly").
- Numbers: a first-person count is fine ("I counted 41 single socks"); no statistics about people in general
  ("Americans lose 50 socks a year") - we have no source for them.
- No fake scarcity ("only today", "last chance").
