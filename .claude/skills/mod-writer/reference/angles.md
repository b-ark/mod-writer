# Hook angles - starting points, not a menu

These are angles we know work for SockLock. They are a floor, not a ceiling: a batch that only uses this list
will look like every batch before it. Use them, and also invent angles of your own (see SKILL.md, Step 2).

| key | Angle | What makes it work |
|-----|-------|--------------------|
| mystery | Lost-sock mystery | A curiosity gap the body answers: where do socks go? |
| confession | Confession / "I tried" | A personal admission, UGC feel, a little embarrassing |
| morning-rush | Morning rush | A concrete moment of chaos: one sock, late, a kid yelling |
| parent | Parent laundry pile | The volume of a family's laundry, the Sunday sort |
| math | Sock math | A first-person count or cost of lost socks |
| hack | Life hack | "A small thing fixed X", without naming the thing |
| humor | Absurd humor | Treat a tiny problem with exaggerated seriousness |
| relationship | Couple / roommate | A small fight or blame over socks |
| gift | Gift | A useful gift that gets a laugh |
| skeptic | Skeptic | "I didn't believe it would work" - honesty buys trust |
| question | Direct question | Ask the viewer about their own basket |

## Where new angles come from
- A different person: a teenager, a grandparent, a roommate, a dog owner, a night-shift nurse, a runner.
- A different moment: packing for a trip, a first date, the gym bag, moving house, a sleepover.
- A different format: a list, a fake news report, a review, a confession to the camera, a countdown, a dare.
- A different feeling: relief, pettiness, nostalgia, pride, mild revenge.

A good new angle is one sentence you could explain to the designer ("the gym bag: one sock always stays in the
locker room") and it still opens on a problem shot the script has, or earns a NEW shot.

## What not to do
- Do not name the mechanism in the hook (magnet, click, snap-on cuffs): save it for the reveal in the body.
- Do not open on a product shot (the reveal, the unboxing, the end card).
- Do not open with price or the brand name.
- Do not stack two angles in one hook.
